# Week 12 Reading Response (draft)

Peggy Wang, "Wang Guangyi: Pop and the People," in *The Future History of Contemporary Chinese Art* (2020)

What I found most interesting is how the author changes the meaning of "Pop." At first, I thought Pop just meant Warhol, bright colors, logos, consumer stuff. But by the end, "Pop" means "popular," as in the people's culture (pp. 112–113). In *Great Criticism: Coca-Cola*, the Coca-Cola logo sits under a worker, a farmer and a soldier. Figures like these came from *baotou*, little propaganda pictures that local artists copied by hand (pp. 104–106). So one side is a brand, and the other side is the people (p. 113). Now I don't see it as just a copy of Western Pop.
